# Tokenomics

This project is designed around a token model that supports governance, staking, and fee-sharing for a cross-chain infrastructure platform.

## Core objectives

The token should:

- incentivize ecosystem participation
- reward relayers, stakers, and liquidity providers
- fund product and protocol development
- align incentives across users, operators, and enterprise customers

## Recommended token roles

### 1. Governance

Token holders vote on:

- protocol upgrades
- treasury allocation
- fee changes
- ecosystem incentives

### 2. Staking

Staking secures the ecosystem by locking tokens for:

- validator / relayer access
- protocol governance weighting
- yield and reward programs

### 3. Fee access / utility

The token can provide:

- reduced platform fees for holders
- premium access to analytics and enterprise tooling
- staking-based routing or message prioritization

## Suggested supply model

```text
Total Supply: 1,000,000,000 BRIDGE

- 20% Team & Founders
- 15% Ecosystem incentives
- 20% Treasury / grants
- 25% Staking rewards
- 10% Community airdrop / early supporters
- 10% Liquidity / market making
```

This allocation should be adjusted after legal review and product priorities are finalized.

## Emission schedule

Recommended approach:

- vesting for team and founders
- gradual emissions for ecosystem incentives
- lower inflation after the network matures

Example:

- Year 1: 8% annual emissions
- Year 2: 6%
- Year 3: 4%
- Year 4+: adaptive governance-based release

## Revenue distribution model

Platform revenue can be distributed as:

- treasury reserve for protocol operations
- staking rewards for token holders
- community grants and ecosystem bounties
- team compensation and development funding

## Risk considerations

- token design must comply with applicable regulations
- staking and rewards should be carefully gas-optimized
- governance should avoid concentration risk
- reserve allocations need transparent treasury management

## Best practice

Use the token primarily as a governance and incentives layer, not as a fake utility token with no real value flow. The token should be reinforced by real platform revenue, platform usage, and operating value.
